## Product Feel

SKLoud should feel compact, trustworthy, civic, and operational. It is a product interface users return to, not a marketing landing page.

## Frontend Patterns To Inspect

- `components.json`
- `package.json`
- `app/assets/css/tailwind.css`
- `app/assets/css/main.css`
- relevant appbar/layout/page/component files

## Design Rules

- Reuse existing components and tokens.
- Preserve light/dark mode.
- Keep navigation compact and responsive.
- Avoid new visual languages, decorative gradients, oversized heroes, and unrelated app shells.
- Include default, hover, active, focus, loading, empty, error, success, and permission states where relevant.
